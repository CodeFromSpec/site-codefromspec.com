## Decisions

Published as a journal entry rather than as a new `/articles`
section. A separate section was considered — the piece is an
analytical essay, closer in kind to the theory than to the
other entries, and the draft lived under `research/drafts/articles/`.
Discarded because a section exists to hold a series, and there
is one piece. Revisit if more analyses follow.

Title changed from "No more reviewing code" to "Could we stop
reviewing code?" before publication. The declarative form
stated the thesis the entry refutes and would circulate alone
in feeds asserting it; the question form is honest standalone
and matches the entry's question-style section titles. Slug
aligned with the title: `could-we-stop-reviewing-code`.
